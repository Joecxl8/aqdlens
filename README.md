# 🇸🇦 AqdLens

**Understand your Saudi employment contract with confidence.**

AqdLens is a multilingual web platform designed to help expatriates in Saudi Arabia better understand their employment contracts, salary structures, end-of-service benefits, and potential contract risks.

> **Aqd** (عقد) means **contract** in Arabic — AqdLens gives you a clearer lens into your employment contract.

## ✨ Features

### 💰 Salary Breakdown
Understand how your compensation is structured, including:

- Basic salary
- Housing allowance
- Transportation allowance
- Other allowances
- Total monthly compensation
- Annual compensation

### 🧮 End-of-Service Benefit Calculator
Estimate your **End-of-Service Benefits (EOSB)** based on information such as:

- Basic salary
- Length of service
- Contract details
- Reason for leaving employment

### 🔍 Contract Risk Analysis
Review employment contract terms and highlight areas that may deserve closer attention.

Examples include:

- Probation periods
- Notice periods
- Salary clauses
- Allowances
- Contract duration
- Termination conditions
- Restrictive clauses

### 🌍 Seven-Language Support
AqdLens is designed for Saudi Arabia's diverse expatriate workforce and supports **seven languages**, making employment information easier to understand.

### 🔐 Privacy-Focused
Employment contracts can contain sensitive personal information. AqdLens is designed with privacy and responsible handling of user data in mind.

## 🎯 Why AqdLens?

Employment contracts can be difficult to understand, especially when working in another country.

AqdLens aims to turn complicated employment information into clear and useful insights so expatriates can better understand:

**What am I being paid?**

**What benefits may I be entitled to?**

**What does this clause mean?**

**Which parts of my contract deserve additional attention?**

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

AqdLens is an informational tool and does **not** provide legal advice.

Calculations, contract analysis, translations, and risk indicators should be treated as estimates or informational guidance. Employment rights and entitlements can depend on individual circumstances and applicable Saudi regulations.

For important employment or legal decisions, users should consult official Saudi government resources or a qualified legal professional.
